==================================================

The project author confirmed on 2026-07-13 that the following bundled project
assets may be redistributed with VARG-Tools because they derive from published
work and are included to reproduce the published software workflow:

- pretrained VARG26 UMAP model files;
- the public/citable VARG26 coordinate/metadata reference CSV;
- the example-data archive;
- user-guide images.

This statement applies to those project-created assets. It does not replace
the separate licenses retained by R, third-party R packages, or other external
software included in a platform bundle.

The distributed reference CSV is generated from the checked Paper 1 source-
status policy. Internal-training, unresolved/withheld, duplicate, and secondary-
standard rows are not included in the app-facing reference table.
